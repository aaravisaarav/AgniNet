WHO & ICDO Wildfire Safety Protocols

World Health Organization & International Civil Defence Organisation

WHO — Smoke & PM2.5 Health Protection
  • Wear Certified Respirators: Use properly fitted N95 / FFP2 masks outdoors; cloth or surgical masks do not block fine wildfire PM2.5 particles.
  • Create a Clean-Air Room: Keep windows and doors tightly sealed, set air conditioners to recirculate mode, and run a HEPA air purifier if available.
  • Protect Vulnerable Groups: Children, elderly individuals, pregnant women, and people with asthma or cardiac conditions should avoid all outdoor exertion and keep rescue inhalers accessible.
ICDO — Civil Defence & Evacuation Protocol
  • Evacuate Early: Never wait to see flames if local authorities issue a precautionary evacuation alert—smoke can reduce road visibility to zero in minutes.
  • Shut Off Utilities: Before leaving, turn off domestic LPG/natural gas cylinders, shut HVAC intakes, and close all interior doors to slow radiant heat spread.
  • 72-Hour Go-Bag: Keep drinking water, ID documents, prescription medicines, torch/powerbank, cotton long-sleeve clothing, and your nation's emergency helpline numbers ready.
